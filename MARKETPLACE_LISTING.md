### 📝 App Overview (Marketplace Detail Page)

Are tasks silently stalling in *"In Review"*, *"Waiting on Client"*, or *"Blocked"* without anyone noticing until it's too late? 

**Bottleneck** is the ultimate performance and SLA management booster for monday.com. It automatically calculates the exact time items spend in every workflow state, highlights critical delays, and gives project managers 1-click tools to resolve blockers before deadlines slip.

---

### 🚀 Key Features

#### 1. The Bottleneck Hub (Stuck Tasks Radar)
- Live table ranking every active item by how long it has been sitting in its current status.
- Color-coded progress indicators show exactly what percentage of the SLA target has been consumed.
- Instant alert badges for **At Risk (75%)** and **Breached (>100%)** tasks.

#### 2. The "1-Click Nudge" Action
- Unblock tasks in seconds without leaving your dashboard.
- Clicking **"1-Click Nudge"** automatically posts a formatted update to the item pulse on monday.com, tagging the assignee to request an immediate status update.

#### 3. Time Tracking Automations (Elevate Precision Management)
- *"X minutes after monday's time tracking column starts running, do this."*
- **Customizable Notifications:** Send real-time alerts when timers hit 30 minutes, 1 hour, or any custom interval.
- **Automated Prioritization:** Move runaway open tickets to the *"Urgent"* group and escalate status to *"Critical"*.
- **Role-Based Targeting:** Direct notifications specifically to Assignees, Project Managers, or Team Leads.

#### 4. Cycle Time & Lead Time Analytics
- Scientific breakdown of **Lead Time** (Creation $\rightarrow$ Done) and **Cycle Time** (In Progress $\rightarrow$ Done).
- Horizontal distribution visualizer highlighting which stage in your sprint consumes the most team capacity.

---

### 🛠️ 2-Minute Quick Setup
1. Add the **Bottleneck** view to any board or dashboard.
2. Under **SLA Configuration**, set maximum duration targets for your key statuses (e.g. *In Review: 24h*).
3. Under **Time Tracking Automations**, choose your notification intervals and escalation recipes.
4. Let Bottleneck monitor your board 24/7 in the background!
